# P2.1 handoff: Personal Library data foundation

**Status:** implemented in the working tree on `dev`, based on `0f0bfa3`, with the acceptance corrections applied. Not committed, pushed, deployed or migrated. Full design: [../personal-library.md](../personal-library.md).

## What exists

- Four additive tables: favorites, recent history, notes, and the owner-approved erasure marker.
- Owner-scoped storage, a strict song identity resolver, and the `withPersonalLibrary` boundary.
- Note deletion through a tombstone in `user_song_notes`, so a retried create cannot bring a deleted note back.
- Account deletion that blocks writes first, handles uncertain Clerk results, and marks completion only after all cleanup succeeds.
- Nightly recovery of interrupted erasures, plus a documented [administrator procedure](../personal-library.md#administrator-recovery-after-an-uncertain-deletion).

## What does not exist yet

No page, navigation, button, editor, public route, view tracking or sheet-file key derivation. Those belong to later checkpoints.

## Known limitations

- History-only songs without durable identity cannot be newly favorited or noted (accepted).
- A disabled account's already-issued token may work until it expires, as it does across the site.
- Note tombstones stay until the account is erased, at most one per song per account, and hold no text.
- Deleted text can remain in PostgreSQL and Neon internals until vacuum and history retention pass.
- An uncertain deletion of an account that still exists stays closed to library writes until resolved through the administrator procedure. Reopening a kept account is a manual owner step.
- Automatic erasure recovery runs on Production only.
- PGlite does not reproduce multi-connection lock contention.
